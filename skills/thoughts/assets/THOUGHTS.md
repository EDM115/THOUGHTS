# THOUGHTS — Things Heard, Observed, Unclear, Guessed, Hacked, Tracked or Suspected : A living document about non-trivial details

This scratchpad preserves non-obvious project knowledge that would prevent a bad decision or costly rediscovery in a fresh thread.  
Record concise conclusions, constraints, consequential decisions and their rationale, verified gotchas, and unresolved questions with enough context to resume safely. Distinguish evidence from hypotheses. Do not store hidden chain-of-thought, transcripts, secrets, or routine progress logs.  
Developers own their entries. The main orchestrator may maintain agent-authored entries according to the selected thoughts command; subagents report candidates to the parent instead of editing this file. Preserve user-authored text unless changes are explicitly authorized.  
Information already authoritative in README, AGENTS, plans, issues, documentation, code, comments, tests, or existing thoughts should not be duplicated here.
